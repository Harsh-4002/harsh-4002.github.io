Privacy Policy

Cat Warriors: Zombie War · Effective date: 27 September 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Cat Warriors: Zombie War (the "Game") handles, why, and the choices you have. The Game is developed by Qissora, an individual developer ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact us at itsharshkantgupta@gmail.com.

In short
  • You don't create an account with us, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress is saved on your device and, if you're signed in to Google Play Games, in your Google Play Games cloud save.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • The Game shows optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is played.
  • We don't sell your information.

1. Information the Game handles

Game progress on your device

The Game stores your progress on your device: levels completed, stars, coins and gems, unlocked cats and power-ups, settings, daily rewards and missions, and an optional in-game nickname you choose. This data stays on your device unless it is backed up through Google Play Games (below).

Google Play Games Services (sign-in and cloud save)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the Game signs you in automatically. Google provides the Game with your Play Games player ID, display name and profile picture, which are shown in the Game. Your game progress (the same data described above) is saved to your Google Play Games "Saved Games" storage, so you keep your progress if you reinstall or change devices. This service is provided by Google and is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Google confirms a purchase to the Game (which product was bought, plus an order ID and purchase token) so the Game can deliver it and restore it later. We do not receive your name, address or payment card details.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game offers optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, device type and settings, IP address (which may indicate approximate location) and how you interact with ads. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

The Game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how players use it, for example which levels are started, won or lost, when rewarded ads are watched, and when purchases are made. This includes device information (such as model and operating system version), an app instance identifier, and your Play Games player ID (an ID issued by Google for this game, not your email or Google account name). We use this only to improve the Game, such as fixing difficulty problems and bugs, and not to identify you personally. See the Firebase privacy information.

2. How we use information

  • To run the Game, save and restore your progress, and deliver and restore purchases.
  • To show optional rewarded ads and give you the rewards you chose.
  • To understand how the Game is played and improve it.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the Game working properly.

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), we rely on: performing our agreement with you (running the Game and delivering purchases), our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the Game, and your consent where required, such as for personalized advertising.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your information. Information is processed by the Google services named above (Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob and Google Firebase), acting on our behalf or under their own terms. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Google Play Games cloud save: until you delete it through Google Play Games.
  • Analytics: kept by Google Firebase according to its retention settings, for no longer than 14 months.
  • Advertising: kept by Google according to its own policies.

5. Your choices

  • Ads: rewarded ads are always optional. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Play Games: you can manage or delete your Play Games profile and saved data in the Google Play Games settings.
  • Local data: uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes the progress stored on your device.
  • Requests: you can contact us to ask about, or ask us to delete, analytics data linked to your Play Games account. Please include your Play Games display name so we can find it. Step-by-step instructions are on our "Delete your data" page.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

7. Security

We rely on the security of Google's services for cloud save, payments, ads and analytics. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we do not collect more information than the Game needs.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game adds features. The effective date above will change, and significant changes will be noted in the Game's store listing.
