Cat Warriors: Zombie War

A mobile game for Android by Qissora

The dogs have turned into zombies, and only the cats stand between them and the last safe zone. Build a squad of cats, place traps and power-ups on the lanes, and hold the barricade against wave after wave of zombie dogs.
