Delete your data — Cat Warriors: Zombie War

Game by Qissora · Last updated 27 September 2026

Cat Warriors: Zombie War doesn't create an account for you. Your information is held in three places: on your phone, in your Google Play Games cloud save, and in the game's analytics (Google Firebase). Here is how to delete each one. The in-game option below handles the first two at once.

Quickest way to ask us: email itsharshkantgupta@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data – Cat Warriors" and include your Google Play Games display name. We will delete the analytics data linked to your Play Games account within 30 days and reply to confirm. Steps 1 and 2 below you can do yourself, straight away.

Easiest: inside the game

  1. Open Cat Warriors: Zombie War and tap the Settings (gear) button on the start screen.
  2. Tap Reset progress, then confirm twice.

This deletes your progress, coins, gems and nickname on your phone and clears your Google Play Games cloud save. If you're offline or not signed in, the cloud save is cleared the next time you sign in. Purchases such as Remove Ads can be restored afterwards with Shop → Restore Purchases.

1. Delete your game progress stored by Google Play Games

  1. Open the Google Play Games app on your phone.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Play Games account & data.
  4. Under Delete individual game data, find Cat Warriors: Zombie War and tap Delete.

This removes the game's cloud save (levels, stars, coins, gems, cats, power-ups, missions and nickname) and your Play Games data for this game.

2. Delete the data on your phone

  1. Uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → Cat Warriors: Zombie War → Storage → Clear storage.

This removes the progress saved on this device.

3. Delete your analytics data

Email us as described above. We delete the gameplay analytics (levels played, ads watched, purchases made) that are linked to your Play Games player ID.

4. Advertising data

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To reset or delete your advertising ID, open Android Settings → Google → Ads and choose Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID on older phones). Data held by Google for advertising is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your game progress (on your phone and in the cloud), your in-game nickname, and analytics data linked to your Play Games account.
  • Kept by Google Play: records of purchases you made, which Google keeps for legal, tax and refund purposes under its own policies. We cannot delete these.
  • Kept anonymously: analytics data that isn't linked to your Play Games account is deleted automatically within 14 months. Totals that can't identify you (for example "how many players finished level 5") may be kept.

Please note: deleting your progress is permanent. Remove Ads and the Starter Pack can be restored with Shop → Restore Purchases, but coins, gems and progress cannot be brought back.
